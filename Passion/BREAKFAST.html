EVERYTHING BAGEL SANDWICH

COOKING TIME: 15 MINUTES/ QUCIK AND EASY/ SERVES 1 PERSON

INGREDIENTS:

  • 1 Everything Bagel
  • 1 Sunnyside Up Egg
  • ½ Avocado
  • Shredded Colby Jack
  • Oscar Mayer Turkey Bacon
  • Salt, Pepper, & Paprika

DIRECTIONS:

  • First, toast your bagel
  • In a pan, cook your sunny-side-up egg with 2 tablespoons of water, cover, and let it cook for about 5 minutes on medium-low heat. Once the egg is steamed, season and place ona bagel
  • Quickly add your cheese. You can let it cool, or immediately put your sliced avocado and cooked turkey bacon
  • Cut in half and Yayy! Delicious sandwich
