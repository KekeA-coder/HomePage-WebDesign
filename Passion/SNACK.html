PUPPY CHOW

COOKING TIME: 10-15 MINUTES/ QUCIK AND EASY/ SERVES 1 PERSON

INGREDIENTS:

  • 1 cup of Cinnamon Toast Crunch (Or Cereal of your choice)
  • ¼ cup of Powdered sugar (Tastes great without it as well)
  • 1/2 cup of Milk Chocolate chips or a bar of chocolate
  • ¼ cup of Butter
  • Oscar Mayer Turkey Bacon
  • ½ Creamy peanut butter

DIRECTIONS:

  • In a bowl, put your chocolate, peanut butter, and butter. Microwave for 30 secs, mix, and put it in for another 30 secs Put your cereal into a ziplock bag and pour your chocolate mix immediately. Shake the bag and make sure every cereal is covered. Put your powdered sugar in the bag and keep shaking it until it’s fully covered Put in the fridge (or freezer if you want it as soon as possible:), I enjoy it when it’s really cold!
