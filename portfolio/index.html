SZA "Drew Barrymore" Poster Series

Project Type: Graphic Design

Tools: Photoshop, Illustrator

Timeline: 2 weeks

Project Brief

Design a series of posters inspired by SZA’s song "Drew Barrymore" that visually communicates themes of vulnerability and self-image.

Process

I started by analyzing the lyrics and emotional tone of the song. Initial sketches focused on composition and typography placement.

Final Outcome

The final posters use muted tones and layered imagery to reflect emotional depth and identity conflict.
