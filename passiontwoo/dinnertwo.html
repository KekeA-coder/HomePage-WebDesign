CARBONARA PASTA

COOKING TIME: 20 MINUTES/ QUCIK AND EASY/ SERVES 1 PERSON

INGREDIENTS:

  • 3 Egg yolks
  • A box of Bucatini noodles
  • 2 cups Parmigiano Reggiano
  • Salt&Pepper, Italian seasoning
  • Turkey Bacon

DIRECTIONS:

  • Boil your pasta for 9 mins (aldentie) with olive oil and salt
  • While the pasta is cooking, cook your bacon in a pan
  • In a bowl, crack 3 egg yolks, mix in the parmiginano and your seasonings Before draining your pasta water, mix in a cooking spoon full on water in the egg mix
  • Before draining your pasta water, mix in a cooking spoon full on water in the egg mix
  • After your pasta is drained in your pot or pan, mix your egg with the pasta and cut up your bacon to place on top, or just mix it in
  • Plate your pasta, and you're all done!
