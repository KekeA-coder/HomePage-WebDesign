    <h4>EVERYTHING BAGEL SANDWICH</h4>
    <img src="https://i.pinimg.com/1200x/ce/88/69/ce8869fb7c226468dfe8c59c5d844940.jpg" alt="everything bagel sandwich" width="500" class="center">

<h3>
    COOKING TIME: 15 MINUTES/
    QUCIK AND EASY/          
    SERVES 1 PERSON
</h3>

 <h4>INGREDIENTS:</h4>
       <ul>
          <li>1 Everything Bagel</li>
          <li>1 Sunnyside Up Egg</li>
          <li>½ Avocado</li>
          <li>Shredded Colby Jack</li>
          <li>Oscar Mayer Turkey Bacon</li>
          <li>Salt, Pepper, & Paprika</li>
       </ul>

  <h4>DIRECTIONS:</h4>     
 <ul>
          <li>First, toast your bagel</li>
          <li>In a pan, cook your sunny-side-up egg with 2 tablespoons of water, cover, and let it cook for about 5 minutes on medium-low heat. Once the egg is steamed, season and place ona  bagel</li>
          <li>Quickly add your cheese. You can let it cool, or immediately put your sliced avocado and cooked turkey bacon</li>
          <li>Cut in half and Yayy! Delicious sandwich</li>
       </ul>
