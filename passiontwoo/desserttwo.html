BROWN SUGAR CHOCOLATE CHIP COOKIE

COOKING TIME: 30 MINUTES/375°F QUCIK AND EASY/ 12 COOKIES

INGREDIENTS:

  • DRY INGREDIENTS
  • 1 ½ cup all-purpose flour
  • ¾ tsp baking soda
  • ¼ tsp salt
  • Semi-sweet chocolate chunks (or chips)
  • WET INGREDIENTS
  • 1 egg
  • 1 Tbsp milk
  • 10 tbsp salted butter
  • ⅔ cup brown sugar
  • ⅓ cup granulated sugar
  • 1 tbsp vanilla extract

DIRECTIONS:

  • First, preheat the oven to 375 F
  • In a pan, put the butter, brown sugar, and granulated sugar. Melt it down and don’t burn it
  • Mix together your dry ingredients in a medium-sized bowl and add your egg, milk, and vanilla. Incorporate the brown sugar mix
  • On a non-stick pan, spoon and ball your dough and place with enough space between cookies. This batch should make a dozen normal-sized cookies. Bake for 10 mins
  • Let them sit till easy to pick up, and ENOUGH!
